SUPER SMASK ZERO

A game by Pigmassacre.

It began in 2016 as a libGDX game called PostWork, built after work and never
finished. The ship, the froglet, the starball, the yellowcrab, the floor, the
drift of it all and the logo's font are the original ones.

Made with Godot Engine (godotengine.org). Its notices are in GODOT_COPYRIGHT.txt.
